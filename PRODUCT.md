# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro static site, Markdown/JSON content, Pages CMS editing, and GitHub Pages deployment. This keeps publishing repository-based while giving the owner a browser-based editing interface without a database or custom admin server.

## Users

Primary visitors are recruiters, investment and finance peers, and intellectually curious readers evaluating the author's reasoning, research process, and ability to challenge conventional views.

## Product Purpose

The site is a personal portfolio and public research notebook. It should make the author's thinking legible through essays, paper notes, strategy visualizations, accounting references, and book studies, while giving returning readers a way to subscribe.

## Positioning

The portfolio emphasizes how a conclusion was reached: assumptions, drivers, counterarguments, evidence, and what would change the view. It is not a conventional resume site or a feed of unsupported opinions.

## Operating Context

The owner normally edits site settings, the About page, articles, images, and attachments through Pages CMS. Pages CMS commits those content changes to GitHub, and GitHub Actions deploys automatically. Direct Markdown editing remains available for bulk or offline work. Newsletter delivery is delegated to a specialist email provider through an embedded subscription form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Article categories, tags, drafts, search, and RSS.
- Browser-based content editing through Pages CMS.
- Image and document uploads stored in the GitHub repository.
- Static hosting compatible with GitHub Pages.
- Subscription form requires a newsletter provider form endpoint before production launch.
- Personal name, biography, social links, and final article text remain open decisions and are centralized for replacement.

## Brand Commitments

- Dynamic and science-fiction influenced, with the interaction quality of the supplied “Nothing to Watch” reference.
- Reading and subscription flows must remain calm, clear, and practical.
- Chinese is the primary language; English terminology may appear where it is natural in finance and research.

## Evidence on Hand

Only topic examples and two website references were supplied. The project must not invent credentials, employers, performance records, subscriber counts, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the work and the thinking, not a generic personal introduction.
2. Make counterarguments and uncertainty visible.
3. Keep publishing simple enough to sustain.
4. Use motion to explain and orient, not to delay reading.
5. Separate real claims from illustrative sample content.
